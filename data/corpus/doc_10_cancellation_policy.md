# Workshop Venue and Event Services Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy

## §1 Standard Cancellation Tiers and Refund Schedules
This policy governs cancellations, refunds, and credits for workshop venue bookings, AV equipment rentals, and associated services contracted through PRISM Facilities across Pune and Mumbai.

Refund eligibility depends on the notice period received in writing prior to the scheduled event start date:
- **Greater than 14 Days Notice (> 14 Calendar Days Prior)**:
  - **Refund**: **100% Full Refund** of all deposited venue rental fees, AV charges, and advance payments.
  - **Deductions**: Zero administrative cancellation fee is applied. Funds are returned in full.
- **Between 7 and 14 Days Notice (7 to 14 Calendar Days Prior)**:
  - **Refund**: **50% Refund** of the total contracted booking value.
  - **Retained Amount**: The remaining 50% is retained by PRISM Facilities to cover reserved hall vacancy and lost booking opportunities.
- **Less than 7 Days Notice (< 7 Calendar Days Prior)**:
  - **Refund**: **0% Refund (No Refund)**.
  - **Forfeiture**: Total booking payments and deposits are completely forfeited due to committed facility staging and calendar lock-in.

## §2 Catering Cancellation Deadlines
Catering services require advance food procurement:
- Catering orders cancelled more than 72 hours prior to the event receive a full refund minus a 10% administrative kitchen fee.
- Catering orders cancelled **within 72 hours of the event are non-refundable**, as bulk food preparations and provisions are already committed.

## §3 Complimentary Rescheduling Policy
To accommodate dynamic corporate schedules, PRISM allows one-time date changes:
- **Single Free Rescheduling**: Every confirmed reservation is permitted to **reschedule once at no cost**, provided written notice is submitted at least **5 business days prior to the original event date**.
- **Rescheduled Date Window**: The new date must fall within **90 calendar days** of the original booking date, subject to venue availability.
- **Subsequent Changes**: Secondary rescheduling requests incur an administrative fee of 15% of the venue daily rate.

## §4 Force Majeure and Exceptional Circumstances
Standard cancellation penalties are waived in full in documented Force Majeure events:
- **Qualifying Circumstances**:
  1. Severe natural disasters, extreme monsoon flooding, or municipal Red Alerts in Pune or Mumbai.
  2. Government-mandated public lockdowns, curfews, or emergency facility closures.
  3. Catastrophic building facility failures (grid electrical outage exceeding 4 hours, water main rupture).
- **Remedy**: Clients receive an immediate **100% full refund** or a 12-month rebooking credit. Client internal schedule changes do not qualify as Force Majeure.

## §5 Refund Processing Mechanics
Approved refunds are processed through PRISM Treasury within 3 business days. Electronic credit card reversals and corporate bank transfers reflect in client accounts within 7 to 10 business days.
