# Corporate Workshop Catering Services, Menus, and Ordering Guidelines

## §1 In-House Catering Packages and Menus
PRISM Hospitality Services provides on-site culinary dining packages for corporate workshops, technical hackathons, and executive meetings hosted at Pune and Mumbai facilities. All packages include morning and afternoon tea service (filter coffee, masala tea, and cookies).

Organizers can select from three in-house meal plans:
- **Vegetarian Thali Package (₹550 per person / day)**: Multi-course meal featuring two seasonal vegetable curries, paneer butter masala, dal tadka, jeera rice, fresh tandoori rotis, raita, salad, and dessert (gulab jamun or kheer). Includes afternoon hot snacks (samosas or veg cutlets).
- **Non-Vegetarian Thali Package (₹750 per person / day)**: Mughlai chicken curry or butter chicken, authentic mutton sukka or rogan josh, seasonal vegetable sabzi, dal makhani, chicken or vegetable biryani, tandoor naans, raita, and dessert (rasmalai). Includes full high-tea refreshments.
- **Continental Executive Buffet (₹850 per person / day)**: Gourmet cold sandwiches, penne pasta in Alfredo or arrabbiata sauce, herb-grilled chicken breast or grilled paneer skewers, garden salad, artisan dinner rolls, mini cheesecakes, and an all-day espresso coffee station.

## §3 Dietary Accommodation and Food Allergen Policy
Special dietary requirements are supported at no additional charge when specified during booking:
- **Vegetarian & Jain**: Strict Jain meals exclude onions, garlic, potatoes, and root vegetables, prepared using isolated cookware.
- **Vegan, Halal, & Gluten-Free**: Designated preparation counters prevent cross-contamination.
- **Severe Allergens**: Individually sealed allergen-safe bento boxes are provided for delegates with nut or shellfish allergies.

## §4 Minimum Order Sizes and 72-Hour Advance Booking Requirement
To ensure kitchen ingredient procurement and chef scheduling:
- **Minimum Order Sizes**: In-house catering requires a **minimum order size of 15 persons**. External caterers require a **minimum headcount of 25 persons**.
- **Advance Booking Requirement (72 Hours)**: All catering packages, menu selections, and final headcounts must be confirmed at least **72 hours in advance** of the event start date.
- **Late Changes**: Headcount additions within 72 hours incur a 20% surcharge and depend on chef capacity. Headcount reductions within 72 hours cannot be refunded.
