# Compressor Warranty Coverage, Terms, and Service Level Agreements

## §1 Standard Factory Warranty Provisions
Every PRISM Model 7 and Model 9 Rotary Screw Compressor includes the manufacturer's Standard Factory Warranty:
- **Warranty Duration**: The standard factory warranty coverage lasts for **24 months from the verified date of installation and commissioning** (or 27 months from initial factory shipment, whichever occurs first).
- **Scope of Coverage**: Full warranty covers free replacement or repair of mechanical and electrical components exhibiting defects in materials, metallurgy, casting, or assembly. Covered items include the rotary screw air-end block, 30 kW drive motor, cooler core, microprocessor controller, and pressure sensors.
- **Labor & Travel**: All service labor and technician travel expenses for approved warranty claims are fully covered with zero customer deductible.

## §2 Extended Warranty Coverage Programs
Customers can purchase extended warranty coverage prior to the standard 24-month expiration:
- **PRISM CarePlus 3-Year Extension**: Extends mechanical and electrical parts coverage to 60 months (5 years) total from commissioning. Includes bi-annual multi-point preventive inspections and free fluid condition lab testing.
- **Air-End Lifetime Security Plan**: Provides a 10-year replacement warranty dedicated to the rotary screw air-end assembly, conditional on active PRISM service contracts and OEM parts.

## §3 Lubricant Mandates and Warranty Voidance Conditions
Warranty protection is strictly dependent on adherence to factory operating standards:
- **Mandatory Lubricant Requirement**: The warranty is **STRICTLY VOID** if non-approved lubricants, generic oils, automotive blends, or mineral oils are used in the fluid circuit. Model 7 compressors require exclusive use of certified **Synthetic PAO ISO 46** lubricant (P/N CMP-OIL-PAO46). Oil samples are tested via spectrometry during warranty claim audits; non-approved oil immediately voids all coverage.
- **Additional Voidance Triggers**: Installing non-OEM spare parts, operating beyond 45°C ambient temperature without approved ventilation, tampering with the 140 PSI relief valve, or operating with Yellow-Status faults beyond 72 continuous hours.

## §4 Service Tier Classifications and Response Times
PRISM Field Support operates 24/7/365 emergency dispatch with guaranteed on-site technician response times:
- **Critical Severity Tier**: Equipment complete shutdown, air-end seizure, or active hazard (smoke/fire). Guaranteed on-site technician response within **4 hours** of ticket creation.
- **Standard Severity Tier**: Equipment running under Yellow Status, continuous knocking under load, or degraded pressure delivery. Guaranteed on-site technician response within **24 hours**.
- **Routine Severity Tier**: Planned preventive maintenance, scheduled oil filter changes, or general technical audits. Guaranteed service response within **72 hours** (3 business days).

## §5 Warranty Claim Lodging Workflow
Submit warranty claims through the PRISM Customer Portal with unit serial number, total running hours, shift logs, and the latest oil analysis lab report. Initial triage completes within 2 business hours.
