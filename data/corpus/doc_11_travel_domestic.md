# Corporate Domestic Travel Reimbursement Policy, Per Diem Allowances, and Approval Procedures

## §1 Domestic Destination City Tier Classifications
To standardize business travel expenditures across regional markets in India, destination cities are classified into three cost-of-living tiers:
- **Tier 1 (Metropolitan Hubs)**: Mumbai (MMR), New Delhi & NCR (Gurugram, Noida), Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, and Kolkata.
- **Tier 2 (Major Urban & Tech Centers)**: Pune, Ahmedabad, Chandigarh, Jaipur, Kochi, Lucknow, Coimbatore, Indore, and Bhubaneswar.
- **Tier 3 (All Other Locations)**: All other district locations, smaller regional towns, and industrial manufacturing sites across India.

## §2 Per Diem Rates and Meal Expense Allowances
Employees traveling on domestic business receive a daily per diem allowance to cover meals and minor incidentals (laundry, local phone calls, tips):
- **Tier 1 Cities**: **₹1,800 per day** (allocable as: Breakfast ₹350, Lunch ₹650, Dinner ₹800).
- **Tier 2 Cities**: **₹1,400 per day** (allocable as: Breakfast ₹300, Lunch ₹500, Dinner ₹600).
- **Tier 3 Cities**: **₹1,000 per day** (allocable as: Breakfast ₹200, Lunch ₹350, Dinner ₹450).

*Meal Deduction Rule*: When meals are provided by a conference, workshop host, or company-paid catering (such as workshop thalis in Doc_09), the employee must deduct the corresponding meal amount from their daily per diem claim.

## §3 Hotel Accommodation Caps and Booking Rules
Lodging must be booked through the PRISM Corporate Travel Portal. Maximum allowable room tariffs per night (exclusive of room taxes/GST):
- **Tier 1 Cities**: Up to **₹6,500 per night**.
- **Tier 2 Cities**: Up to **₹4,500 per night**.
- **Tier 3 Cities**: Up to **₹3,000 per night**.

Minibar, alcohol, and pay-per-view entertainment charges are personal expenses and will not be reimbursed.

## §4 Local Transportation and Taxi / Cab Policy
Intra-city transit reimbursement guidelines:
- **Airport and Rail Transfers**: Pre-paid airport taxi counters, Uber Premier, or Ola Prime are fully reimbursable against digital receipt.
- **Daily Commutes and Client Visits**: Employees should use standard ride-hailing services (Uber Sedan/Go, Ola, or metered auto-rickshaws).
- **Full-Day Cab Rentals**: Hiring a daily rental car (8 hours / 80 km) requires advance written approval from the Department Manager, limited to multi-site audit itineraries.
- **Personal Vehicle Usage**: Driving a personal car on business is reimbursed at **₹10.00 per km for cars** and **₹5.00 per km for two-wheelers**, plus documented tolls.

## §5 Receipt Requirements, Submission Deadlines, and Approval Workflow
- **Receipt Mandates**: Itemized tax invoices with GSTIN are mandatory for hotel stays, flights, trains, and any single transit fare over ₹200. Flat-rate per diem meals under daily limits do not require individual receipts.
- **Submission Deadline (30 Days)**: All domestic expense claims must be submitted in the expense portal within **30 calendar days** from the final date of travel. Unsubmitted claims after 30 days are automatically rejected.
- **Approval Workflow Hierarchy**:
  1. **Line Manager**: Reviews business justification and claims up to ₹50,000.
  2. **Department Head / Director**: Required sign-off for claims between ₹50,000 and ₹1,50,000.
  3. **Finance Audit Desk**: Verifies receipts and issues payment via direct payroll deposit within 5 business days.
