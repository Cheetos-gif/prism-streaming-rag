# Workshop Venue Booking Directory, Capacity Guidelines, and Pricing (Pune & Mumbai)

## §1 Corporate Workshop Venues Overview
PRISM Facilities & Event Services operates specialized executive event and training workshop venues across Pune and Mumbai. These corporate spaces host technical seminars, hackathons, client workshops, and training courses. All facilities feature commercial-grade climate control, ergonomic furnishings, dedicated gigabit internet, and on-site AV technicians. Venues can be reserved for full-day (08:30 to 18:30 IST) or half-day sessions.

## §2 Venue A: Hinjewadi IT Park, Pune (Capacity 20-40)
- **Seating Capacity**: **20 to 40 participants** (optimized for 30 attendees in interactive classroom, U-shape, or team cluster layouts).
- **Daily Booking Price**: **₹28,000 per day** (+ 18% GST). Half-day booking rate is ₹16,000.
- **Audiovisual (AV) Equipment**: Dual wall-mounted 75-inch commercial 4K LED interactive touchscreen displays, wireless HDMI screencasting adapters (AirPlay and Miracast), ceiling beamforming microphone array, 1080p pan-tilt-zoom (PTZ) optical camera, ceiling sound speakers, and high-speed Wi-Fi 6 (300 Mbps symmetrical leased line).
- **Parking Availability**: 25 dedicated reserved parking spaces in the secure basement garage; visitor bays available on first-come basis.

## §3 Venue B: Kothrud Commercial Complex, Pune (Capacity 40-80)
- **Seating Capacity**: **40 to 80 participants** (accommodates 50 to 60 delegates comfortably in theatre, banquet, or dual-cluster setups).
- **Daily Booking Price**: **₹45,000 per day** (+ 18% GST). Half-day booking rate is ₹26,000.
- **Audiovisual (AV) Equipment**: 150-inch motorized widescreen laser projection display, digital interactive speaker podium, 2 UHF wireless handheld microphones plus 2 wireless lapel condenser mics, digital audio mixing console with feedback suppression, and dual-redundant 500 Mbps fiber links.

## §4 Venue C: Baner High Street, Pune (Capacity 15-25)
- **Seating Capacity**: **15 to 25 participants** (designed for executive roundtables and focused technical design squads).
- **Daily Booking Price**: **₹18,500 per day** (+ 18% GST).

## §6 Booking Timelines, Security Deposits, and Facility Rules
- **Reservation Lead Time**: Booking requests must be submitted through the PRISM Corporate Services Portal at least 5 business days in advance.
- **Deposit Policy**: A 25% non-refundable reservation deposit is required upon booking confirmation; remaining 75% balance is due 7 business days prior to event date.
- **Operating Curfew**: Standard access runs 08:30 to 18:30 IST. Overtime facility usage costs ₹3,500 per hour.
