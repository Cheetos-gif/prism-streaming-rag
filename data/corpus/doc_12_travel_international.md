# International Travel Policy, Senior Director Approvals, Foreign Currency Verification, and Exceptions

## §1 Mandatory Pre-Approval by Senior Director
Cross-border business travel requires strict executive authorization prior to making any financial commitments:
- **Travel Authorization Request (TAR)**: Employees planning international travel must submit an electronic TAR through the Global Mobility Portal at least **21 calendar days** before departure.
- **Mandatory Senior Director Approval**: No international flights, hotels, visa fees, or conference registrations may be booked without prior explicit written and digital approval from the **Senior Director (or Vice President)** of the employee's operating business unit.
- **Unauthorized Bookings**: Any international travel booked without prior Senior Director sign-off is deemed an unauthorized personal expenditure and will not be reimbursed by the company.

## §2 International City Tier Zones and Per Diem Allowances
Daily per diem rates cover meals and minor daily incidentals (local SIM/data, tips, transit):
- **Zone A (High-Cost Global Metros)**: New York City, San Francisco, London, Paris, Zurich, Tokyo, Singapore.
  - **Daily Per Diem**: **$110 USD / €100 EUR / £85 GBP per day**.
  - **Hotel Nightly Ceiling**: Up to **$280 USD per night**.
- **Zone B (Standard International Cities)**: Seoul, Dubai, Frankfurt, Madrid, Sydney, Taipei, Milan.
  - **Daily Per Diem**: **$85 USD per day**.
  - **Hotel Nightly Ceiling**: Up to **$200 USD per night**.
- **Zone C (Developing & Regional Markets)**: Bangkok, Kuala Lumpur, Nairobi, Sao Paulo, Manila.
  - **Daily Per Diem**: **$65 USD per day**.
  - **Hotel Nightly Ceiling**: Up to **$140 USD per night**.

## §3 Mandatory Foreign Currency Receipt Verification
Due to statutory tax and cross-border audit compliance:
- **Receipt Verification Mandatory**: Every single foreign business expenditure (excluding standard flat-rate per diem meals) **MUST be supported by an original itemized digital receipt or paper invoice** detailing vendor name, transaction date, local currency amount, and tax. Credit card summaries without itemization will be rejected.
- **Currency Conversion Rules**: Credit card transactions must reconcile against the official card statement exchange rate. Foreign cash transactions are converted using the official historical OANDA mid-market interbank exchange rate on the date of transaction.

## §4 Post-Travel Booking Exception Protocol
In critical field emergencies (e.g., immediate factory equipment outages or urgent field technician dispatches) where advance TAR submission is physically impossible:
- **Written Justification Dossier**: The employee must submit a written justification detailing the emergency nature of the travel and proof of operational necessity.
- **7-Day Senior Director Approval Window**: The written justification dossier and post-travel expense report must secure formal **Senior Director approval within exactly 7 calendar days** of the employee's return to the home country.
- **Forfeiture**: If Senior Director approval is not obtained within 7 days, the exception is voided and the employee remains personally liable for all travel costs.

## §5 Mandatory International Travel Insurance
All employees on international assignments are enrolled in the PRISM Global Travel & Medical Protection Policy (Allianz Global Assistance):
- **Medical Coverage**: Up to $500,000 USD for emergency medical care, hospitalization, and emergency evacuation.
- **Travel Protection**: Covers flight delays, lost passports, and baggage delays ($500 USD allowance after 12-hour delay).
- **Mandate**: Employees must keep the 24/7 Global SOS assistance contact (+1-800-555-PRISM) on their mobile device during travel.
