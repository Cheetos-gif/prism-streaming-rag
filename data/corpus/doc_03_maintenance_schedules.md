# Compressor Preventive Maintenance Schedules and Operational Run Policies

## §1 Daily Operational Inspection Checklist
Every Model 7 compressor must undergo daily inspection by certified plant maintenance operators before each shift:
- **Lubricant Level Check**: Inspect the oil sight glass on the separator vessel while running loaded. Fluid level must be between 50% and 75% of the visual indicator.
- **Pressure Gauge Verification**: Confirm the discharge pressure gauge reads nominal 125 PSI (±5 PSI). Verify pressure vents down to ~25 PSI during unloaded idle.
- **Air Filter Indicator**: Check the pop-up restriction indicator on the air intake. Service the filter cartridge if the yellow/red indicator flag trips.
- **Moisture Drain Check**: Verify automatic condensate drain solenoid vents accumulated water without blockage.

## §2 Weekly Preventive Maintenance Routines
Weekly maintenance checks are scheduled at the end of each operational week (every Friday):
- **Condensate Drain**: Open manual receiver drain valve to flush moisture and sediment.
- **Belt Tension and Couplings**: Measure belt deflection (8–10 mm under 50 N center load) on auxiliary drives; inspect flexible couplings for elastomeric wear.
- **Cooler Core Cleaning**: Blow clean dry compressed air backward through aluminum radiator fins to remove ambient dust.

## §3 Monthly Servicing and Fluid Health Evaluation
Monthly maintenance focuses on fluid chemistry and consumable replacement:
- **Oil Sample Analysis**: Extract a 100 mL oil sample for laboratory testing of kinematic viscosity (ISO 46), Total Acid Number (TAN < 2.0 mg KOH/g), and wear metals.
- **Filter Replacement**: Replace the spin-on oil filter element (CMP7-FLT-002) every 1,000 running hours or monthly.
- **Electrical Terminals**: Inspect 30 kW motor contactors and retorque electrical power lugs to 4.5 Nm.

## §4 Quarterly Comprehensive System Inspection
Quarterly maintenance requires a planned 4-hour service outage by authorized PRISM technicians:
- **Full System Inspection**: Overhaul the 71°C thermostatic mixing valve, test minimum pressure check valve, and pop-test the 140 PSI safety relief valve.
- **Air-Oil Separator**: Evaluate separator differential pressure; replace cartridge (CMP7-SEP-004) if differential exceeds 0.8 bar.
- **Motor Regreasing**: Inject 15 grams of high-temperature polyurea grease into motor bearing zerk fittings.

## §5 Run-Till-Failure and Continued Operation Policy
PRISM operational policy permits continued operation of equipment experiencing minor faults ("Run-Till-Failure" operational deferral) ONLY when ALL criteria are satisfied:
1. **Next Maintenance Proximity**: The next scheduled preventive maintenance or planned service window is confirmed **within 5 business days** (e.g., fault identified mid-week running until Friday planned shutdown).
2. **Non-Critical Classification**: The fault is formally classified as a **non-critical minor fault** (Yellow Status), such as mild startup knocking that subsides, minor oil weeping (< 5 drops/min), or slight belt vibration.
3. **Safety Thresholds Intact**: The compressor must NOT exhibit critical red-status faults, such as continuous knocking under load, burning smell, smoke, or temperatures over 100°C. Any critical fault instantly revokes this policy, requiring immediate shutdown.
